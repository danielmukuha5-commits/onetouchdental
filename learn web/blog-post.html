Braces in Thika: Metallic vs Ceramic — Which Should You Choose?

Published on April 30, 2026 • 7 min read

Choosing the right braces is an important decision for your smile and confidence. At One Touch Dental Clinic in Thika, we offer both metallic and ceramic braces. Here's a comprehensive guide to help you decide which option is best for you.

Metallic Braces: The Traditional Choice

Metallic braces have been the standard for orthodontic treatment for decades. They consist of metal brackets and wires that gradually move your teeth into proper alignment.

Pros of Metallic Braces:

  • Cost-effective: KES 30,000 per jaw (KES 50,000 for both jaws)
  • Durable: Less likely to break during treatment
  • Effective: Can handle complex orthodontic cases
  • Fast results: Usually complete treatment in 18-24 months

Cons of Metallic Braces:

  • Appearance: More noticeable than ceramic braces
  • Discomfort: May cause more irritation to gums and cheeks initially

Ceramic Braces: The Discreet Option

Ceramic braces use clear or tooth-colored brackets that blend with your natural teeth, making them much less noticeable than metallic braces.

Pros of Ceramic Braces:

  • Aesthetic: Much less visible than metal braces
  • Comfortable: Smoother brackets reduce irritation
  • Effective: Same orthodontic results as metal braces

Cons of Ceramic Braces:

  • Cost: KES 35,000 per jaw (KES 50,000 for both jaws)
  • Fragile: Brackets can break if you eat hard foods
  • Staining: May stain if you drink coffee, tea, or smoke
  • Treatment time: May take slightly longer (20-26 months)

Which Should You Choose?

The best choice depends on your lifestyle, budget, and aesthetic preferences:

Choose Metallic Braces if:

  • You want the most cost-effective option
  • You need treatment for complex cases
  • You're not concerned about the appearance

Choose Ceramic Braces if:

  • Aesthetics are important to you
  • You have a job or social situation where appearance matters
  • You're willing to pay a bit more for discretion
