Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Patients Always Ask

How much do braces cost in Thika? +
At One Touch Dental, metallic braces cost KES 30,000 per jaw and ceramic braces KES 35,000 per jaw. If you need both jaws aligned, the price is KES 50,000 — saving you KES 10,000-20,000 compared to doing them separately. We offer flexible payment plans. Book a KES 200 consultation for your exact personalised quote.
Do you see dental emergencies the same day? +
Yes — always. We never turn away someone in pain. Call us on 0708 792043 and we will fit you in the same day. Don't wait with tooth pain — infections spread fast and what starts as a simple fix can become an expensive problem very quickly.
Is root canal treatment painful? +
This is the biggest misconception in dentistry. Root canal treatment relieves pain — it doesn't cause it. You are given local anaesthetic throughout the procedure, which itself is no more uncomfortable than a filling. At KES 5,000 per tooth, you'll walk out pain-free with your natural tooth saved. Most patients are genuinely surprised at how comfortable the experience is.
What is the cost of a dental consultation in Thika? +
A full oral examination at One Touch Dental costs just KES 200. This is a comprehensive checkup where our dentist assesses your teeth, identifies any issues, and gives you a detailed treatment plan with exact pricing — before anything else is done. No commitment, no pressure.
How much does teeth cleaning cost in Thika? +
Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) at One Touch Dental costs KES 2,000. This removes tartar buildup, surface stains, and bacteria that brushing can't reach. Most patients say their mouth feels fresher and cleaner than it has in years. We recommend it every 6 months.
Do you offer payment plans for braces? +
Yes. We understand that KES 30,000–50,000 is a significant investment. We offer flexible instalment plans so you can start your braces journey without paying everything upfront. Ask our team during your KES 200 consultation and we'll find a plan that works for your budget.
Do you treat children at One Touch Dental? +
Absolutely. We welcome patients of all ages. Our team is experienced with children and knows how to make young patients feel safe, calm, and comfortable. We recommend children have their first dental visit by age 3, and regular checkups every 6 months after that.
What are dental ornaments and how much do they cost? +
Dental ornaments (also called tooth gems) are small decorative accessories that are safely bonded to the surface of your tooth — no drilling required. They're a popular way to add a unique touch to your smile. At One Touch Dental, ornaments cost KES 1,000 per ornament and are completely reversible.
